Question:
[math]\frac{1}{1+2 i}[/math]এর জটিল অনুবন্ধী নিচের কোনটি?

Options:
ক. <span class="math-tex">\( \frac{1}{2 i-1}\)</span>
খ. <span class="math-tex">\( 1-2 \mathrm{i}\)</span>
গ. <span class="math-tex">\(\frac{1+2 \mathrm{i}}{5}\)</span>
ঘ. <span class="math-tex">\(\frac{1-2 \mathrm{i}}{5}\)</span>


Correct Answer: গ

Solution:
\(\frac{1}{1+2 \mathrm{i}}\)এর জটিল অনুবন্ধী\(\begin{gathered}\text { }=\frac{1}{1-2 \mathrm{i}}=\frac{1+2 \mathrm{i}}{(1-2 \mathrm{i})(1+2 \mathrm{i})} \\=\frac{1+2 \mathrm{i}}{1+4}=\frac{1+2 \mathrm{i}}{5}\end{gathered}\)

Question:
 যদি  [math]\frac{2-i}{2+i}=A+i B[/math] হলে, A এর মান কোনটি? 

Options:
ক. <span class="math-tex">\(1\)</span>
খ. <span class="math-tex">\(\frac{4}{5}\)</span>
গ. <span class="math-tex">\(\frac{3}{5}\)</span>
ঘ. <span class="math-tex">\( -\frac{4}{5}\)</span>


Correct Answer: গ

Solution:
\(\mathrm{A}+\mathrm{iB}=\frac{2-\mathrm{i}}{2+\mathrm{i}}=\frac{(2-\mathrm{i})^2}{2^2-\mathrm{i}^2}\\=\frac{4-4 \mathrm{i}+\mathrm{i}^2}{4+1}=\frac{4-4 \mathrm{i}-1}{5} =\frac{3}{5}-\frac{4}{5} \mathrm{i}\\ \therefore A=\frac{3}{5}\)
