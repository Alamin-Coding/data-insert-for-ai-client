Options:
ক. <span class="math-tex">\(-1\)</span>
খ. <span class="math-tex">\(-i\)</span>
গ. <span class="math-tex">\(1\)</span>
ঘ. <span class="math-tex">\(i\)</span>

Solution:
\(^2+\mathrm{i}^2+\mathrm{i}^3+\ldots+\mathrm{i}^{23}\\=\frac{\mathrm{i}\left(1-\mathrm{i}^{23}\right)}{1-\mathrm{i}}=\frac{\mathrm{i}(1+\mathrm{i})}{1-\mathrm{i}}=\mathrm{i}^2=-1\)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Question:
যদি [math]\left(\frac{1+i}{1-i}\right)^n=1[/math]  হয়, তবে [math]n[/math]  এর সর্বনিম্ন অখণ্ড মান- 

Options:
ক. <span class="math-tex">\(2\)</span>
খ. <span class="math-tex">\(3\)</span>
গ. <span class="math-tex">\(4\)</span>
ঘ. <span class="math-tex">\(6\)</span>


Correct Answer: গ

Solution:
\(\left(\frac{1+\mathrm{i}}{1-\mathrm{i}}\right)^{\mathrm{n}}=1\\ \Rightarrow\left(\frac{1+2 \mathrm{i}+\mathrm{i}^2}{1^2-\mathrm{i}^2}\right)^n=1\\ \Rightarrow\left(\frac{2 \mathrm{i}}{2}\right)^{\mathrm{n}}=1 \Rightarrow \mathrm{i}^{\mathrm{n}}=1\\ \Rightarrow\left(\mathrm{i}^4\right)^{\frac{\mathrm{n}}{4}}=1\\ \Rightarrow 1^{\frac{\mathrm{n}}{4}}=1 \Rightarrow \frac{\mathrm{n}}{4}=1 \therefore \mathrm{n}=4\)
