খ. <span class="math-tex">\(-1 \pm \sqrt{2}\)</span>

Question:
যদি [math]\mathrm{z}_1=1-i, z_2=\sqrt{3}+i[/math]  হয়, তবে [math]\frac{z_2}{z_1}[/math]  এর  নতি-

Options:
ক. <span class="math-tex">\(\frac{5 \pi}{12}\)</span>
খ. <span class="math-tex">\(\frac{\pi}{6}\)</span>
গ. <span class="math-tex">\(-\frac{\pi}{4}\)</span>
ঘ. <span class="math-tex">\(-\frac{5 \pi}{12}\)</span>


Correct Answer: ক

Solution:
\(\frac{z_2}{z_1}=\frac{\sqrt{3}+i}{1-i} \)এর নতি\(\begin{aligned}&amp; =\arg (\sqrt{3}+i)-\arg (1-i)=\tan ^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\right)-\tan ^1\left(\frac{-1}{1}\right) \\&amp; =\tan ^{-1}\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\right)+\tan ^{-1}(1)=\frac{\pi}{6}+\frac{\pi}{4}=\frac{5 \pi}{12}\end{aligned}\)
