Question:
[math]|5-2 x| \leq 4[/math] অসমতাটির সমাধান- 

Options:
ক. <span class="math-tex">\(-1 \leq x \leq 9\)</span>
খ. <span class="math-tex">\(\frac{1}{2} \leq x \leq \frac{9}{2}\)</span>
গ. <span class="math-tex">\( x \leq-\frac{1}{2}\ or\ x \geq \frac{9}{2}\)</span>
ঘ. <span class="math-tex">\(-\frac{1}{2}&lt;x&lt;\frac{9}{2}\)</span>


Correct Answer: খ

Solution:
\(|5-2 x| \leq 4 \Rightarrow-4 \leq 5-2 x \leq 4\\\Rightarrow 4 \geq 2 \mathrm{x}-5 \geq-4\\ \Rightarrow 9 \geq 2 \mathrm{x} \geq 1\\ \Rightarrow \frac{9}{2} \geq \mathrm{x} \geq \frac{1}{2} \therefore \frac{1}{2} \leq \mathrm{x} \leq \frac{9}{2}\)
