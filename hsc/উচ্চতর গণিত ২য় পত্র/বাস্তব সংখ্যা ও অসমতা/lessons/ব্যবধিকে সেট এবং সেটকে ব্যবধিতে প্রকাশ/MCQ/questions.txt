Question:
 [math]\frac{1}{|3 x-1|}&gt;1[/math] এর সমাধান হলো- 

Options:
ক. <span class="math-tex">\(\left(-\infty, \frac{1}{3}\right) \cup(1, \infty)\)</span>
খ. <span class="math-tex">\(x&gt;\frac{1}{3}\)</span>
গ. <span class="math-tex">\(0&lt;x&lt;\frac{2}{3}\)</span>
ঘ. <span class="math-tex">\(\left(0, \frac{1}{3}\right) \cup\left(\frac{1}{3}, \frac{2}{3}\right)\)</span>


Correct Answer: ঘ

Solution:
\(\frac{1}{|3 x-1|}&gt;1 \Rightarrow 3 x-1 
eq 0 \Rightarrow x 
eq \frac{1}{3}.\)আবার, \(|3 \mathrm{x}-1|&lt;1 \Rightarrow-1&lt;3 \mathrm{x}-1&lt;1\)\(\Rightarrow-1+1&lt;3 \mathrm{x}&lt;2 \Rightarrow 0&lt;3 \mathrm{x}&lt;2\)\(\therefore \)ডোমেন\(0&lt;\mathrm{x}&lt;\frac{2}{3}. যখন \mathrm{x} 
eq \frac{1}{3}\)\text { সমাধান }=\left(0, \frac{1}{3}\right) \cup\left(\frac{1}{3}, \frac{2}{3}\right)
