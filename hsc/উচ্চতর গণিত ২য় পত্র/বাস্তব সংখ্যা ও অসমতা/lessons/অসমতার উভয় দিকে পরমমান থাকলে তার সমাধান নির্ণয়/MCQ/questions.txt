Question:
বাস্তব সংখ্যায় [math]|x+2|&lt;|4 x+1|[/math]  এর সমাধান সেট কোনটি?

Options:
ক. <span class="math-tex">\(\left(-\infty,-\frac{3}{5}\right] \cup\left(\frac{1}{3}, \infty\right) \)</span>
খ. <span class="math-tex">\(\left(-\infty,-\frac{3}{5}\right) \cup\left[\frac{1}{3}, \infty\right) \)</span>
গ. <span class="math-tex">\( \left(-\infty,-\frac{3}{5}\right) \cup\left(\frac{1}{3}, \infty\right) \)</span>
ঘ. <span class="math-tex">\(\left(-\frac{3}{5}, \frac{1}{3}\right) \)</span>


Correct Answer: গ

Solution:
\( |x+2|&lt;|4 x+1| \Rightarrow x^2+4 x+4&lt;16 x^2+8 x+1\\\Rightarrow 15 \mathrm{x}^2+4 \mathrm{x}-3&gt;0\\ \Rightarrow 15 \mathrm{x}^2+9 \mathrm{x}-5 \mathrm{x}-3&gt;0\\\Rightarrow(3 \mathrm{x}-1)(5 \mathrm{x}+3)&gt;0\\ \Rightarrow\left(\mathrm{x}-\frac{1}{3}\right)\left(\mathrm{x}+\frac{3}{5}\right)&gt;0\)\(\therefore \mathrm{x}&gt;\frac{1}{3}\) অথবা, \(\mathrm{x}&lt;-\frac{3}{5} \)वा, \(\left(-\infty,-\frac{3}{5}\right) \cup\left(\frac{1}{3}, \infty\right)\)

Question:
|x-5|=5  হলে,  x  এর মান কত?

Options:
ক. 10
গ. 0,-10&nbsp;
ঘ. 10,0


Correct Answer: ঘ

Solution:
\( |x-5|=5 \Rightarrow x^2-10 x+25=25\\\Rightarrow \mathrm{x}^2-10 \mathrm{x}=0\\ \Rightarrow \mathrm{x}(\mathrm{x}-10)=0 \therefore \mathrm{x}=10,0\)
