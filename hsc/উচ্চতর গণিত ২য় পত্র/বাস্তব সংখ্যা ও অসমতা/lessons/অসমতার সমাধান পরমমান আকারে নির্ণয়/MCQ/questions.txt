Question:
 [math]-2&lt;x&lt;5[/math]  অসমতাটি পরমমানে লেখা যায়: 

Options:
ক. <span class="math-tex">\(|x-3|&lt;8\)</span>
খ. <span class="math-tex">\(|2 \mathrm{x}-3|&lt;7\)</span>
গ. <span class="math-tex">\(|2 x+3|&lt;7\)</span>
ঘ. <span class="math-tex">\( |2 \mathrm{x}-3|&lt;8\)</span>


Correct Answer: খ

Solution:
\(\begin{aligned} &amp; \text { }-2&lt;x&lt;5,\left[\because \frac{-2+5}{2}=\frac{3}{2}\right] \\ &amp; \Rightarrow-2-\frac{3}{2}&lt;x-\frac{3}{2}&lt;5-\frac{3}{2} \Rightarrow-\frac{7}{2}&lt;x-\frac{3}{2}&lt;\frac{7}{2} \\ &amp; \Rightarrow-7&lt;2 x-3&lt;7 \quad \therefore|2 x-3|&lt;7\end{aligned}\)

Question:
[math]​​​​​​​ x^2-8 x&lt;33[/math] হলে, x এর মান হবে- 

Options:
ক. <span class="math-tex">\(-3&lt;x&lt;11\)</span>
খ. <span class="math-tex">\(11&lt;x&lt;22\)</span>

ঘ. <span class="math-tex">\(-1&lt;x&lt;2\)</span>


Correct Answer: ক

Solution:
\(x^2-8 x&lt;33 \Rightarrow x^2-8 x-33&lt;0\\\begin{aligned}&amp; \Rightarrow \mathrm{x}^2-11 \mathrm{x}+3 \mathrm{x}-33&lt;0 \\&amp; \Rightarrow \mathrm{x}(\mathrm{x}-11)+3(\mathrm{x}-11)&lt;0 \\&amp; \Rightarrow(\mathrm{x}-11)(\mathrm{x}+3)&lt;0 \quad\\ &amp;\therefore-3&lt;\mathrm{x}&lt;11\end{aligned}\)

Question:

ক. <span class="math-tex">\( |2 x-5| \leq 4\)</span>
