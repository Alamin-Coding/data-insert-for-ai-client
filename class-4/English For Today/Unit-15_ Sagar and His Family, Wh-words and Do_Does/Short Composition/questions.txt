Question:
Write a short composition in five sentences about 'Your Grandparents'. (Remember to use capital letters, full stops, other punctuation marks and correct spelling.)

Solution:
My Grandparents (আমার দাদা-দাদি)
My grandfather's name is Hasibur Rahman. My grandmother's name is Farida Begum. Both my grandparents are alive. My grandfather was a businessman. My grandmother is a housewife. She prepares delicious pithas for me. My grandparents love me very much. They play with me and tell me many stories.
