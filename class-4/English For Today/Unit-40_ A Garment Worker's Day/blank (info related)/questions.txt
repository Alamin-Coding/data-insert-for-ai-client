Question:
I am a student. I get up from bed at (a) _____. I take my breakfast at (b) _____. I start for school at (c) _____. I attend the national anthem at (d) _____. Our first class starts at (e) _____.

Solution:
a) 6:00 a.m.; b) 6:30 a.m.; c) 7:00 a.m.; d) 7:35 a.m.; e) 8:00 a.m.
