Question:
Write a short composition in five sentences about "A Garment Worker".

Solution:
A Garment Worker
A garment worker works in a readymade garments factory. He/She does the work of making different clothes. He/She works hard. But he/she gets little in return. The financial condition of his/her family is poor. He/She works to help her family financially. He/She sometimes works extra hours to make more money. He/She can't spend her money whenever he/she wants, because his/her family needs this money for food and other things.
