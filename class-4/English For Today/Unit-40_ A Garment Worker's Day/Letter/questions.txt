Question:
You are Hasan. You are going on a picnic to Cox's Bazar. You want to take your friend Arif with you. Now, write a letter to your friend inviting him to join a picnic.

Solution:
Chattogram
January 20, 2025
Dear Arif,
How are you? You will be glad to know that we are going to Cox's Bazar on 28th instant to enjoy a picnic. We all are interested to take you with us. If you are with us, we can enjoy the whole of the picnic. On the way, we shall do many things and enjoy many sights. So, you are invited to go to the picnic with us. Please, come to our house a day earlier.
More when we meet. Come in time.
Yours ever,
Hasan
