Question:
Suppose, you are Joli. You went to visit Kuakata. Write a letter to your friend Khadiza about your visit.

Solution:
Uttara, Dhaka
1 December, 2025
Khadiza!
I am just back from Kuakata. It was a nice visit. I am going to describe it now.
Kuakata is a very beautiful place. It has a sandy beach. It is about 30 kilometres long and 6 kilometres wide. We saw both the sunrise and the sunset from the beach. It was a wonderful experience. I am really happy to experience such a nice journey.
No more today. More when we meet.
With love,
Joli
