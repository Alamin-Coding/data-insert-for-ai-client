Question:
Limon is a student of Class 3. He gets up at (a) _____. Then he brushes his teeth and takes a bath. He has his breakfast at (b) _____. He starts for school at (c) _____. He comes back home at (d) _____. Then he goes to the playground at (e) _____.

Solution:
(a) 6 a.m.; (b) 7 a.m.; (c) 8 a.m.; (d) 12 p.m.; (e) 5 p.m.
