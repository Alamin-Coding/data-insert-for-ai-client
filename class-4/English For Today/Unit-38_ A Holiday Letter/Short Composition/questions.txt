Question:
Write a short composition about "Your Visit to Cox's Bazar".
a) When did you go to Cox's Bazar?
b) Whom did you go there with?
c) How did you go there?
d) How many days did you pass there?
e) Why did you go to Cox's Bazar?

Solution:
My Visit to Cox's Bazar
I went to Cox's Bazar last month. I went there on the occasion of summer vacation. I went there with my family members. We went there by bus. We passed three days in Cox's Bazar. I went there because I wanted to visit the longest beach of the world.
