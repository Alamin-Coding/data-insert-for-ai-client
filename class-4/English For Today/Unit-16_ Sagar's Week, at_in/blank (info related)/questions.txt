Question:
A school teacher goes to his workplace at _____. Не starts teaching at _____. He has a tiffin break at _____ and lunch break at _____. He teaches his students till _____. He returns home at _____.

Solution:
A school teacher goes to his workplace at 7:30 am. He starts teaching at 8:00 am. He has a tiffin break at 11 am. and lunch break at 1:30 pm. He teaches his students till 3:30 pm. He returns home at 4:00 pm.
