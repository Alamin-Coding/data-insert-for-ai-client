Question:
Write a short composition about "Your Daily Routine" by answering the following questions.
a) When do you get up in the morning?
b) When do you leave for school?
c) What do you do in the evening?
d) What do you do after finishing your homework?
e) When do you go to bed?

Solution:
My Daily Routine
I get up at 6 o'clock in the morning. After taking breakfast at 8 a.m.; I leave for school at 9 a.m. In the evening, I complete my homework. After finishing the homework, I spend time with my grandparents. Then I go to bed at 10 p.m.
