Question:
Suppose, you are Toma. Your friend Nasima wrote you a letter about her weekdays. Now, write a letter to your friend about how you spend your weekdays.

Solution:
Dhaka
April 12, 2025
Dear Nasima,
I've received your letter just now. In your letter, you've written about how you spend your weekdays. I'm also giving you a short description of my weekdays.
On my weekdays, I wake up at 7 a.m. Then I have my breakfast at 7:30 a.m. My school starts at 9 o'clock. So, I start for school at 8:30 a.m. In the afternoon, I come back home from school. I study for three hours at night. Then I watch TV for about half an hour. I have my dinner at 10 p.m. At night, I go to bed at 10:30 o'clock.
No more today. Write me soon.
Your friend,
Toma
