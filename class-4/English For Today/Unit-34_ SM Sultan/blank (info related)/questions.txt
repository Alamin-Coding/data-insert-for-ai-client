Question:
My mother wakes up early at (a) _____. She says her prayer at (b) _____. Then, she starts cleaning house till (c) _____. She prepares breakfast for the family at (d) _____. She takes her breakfast at (e) _____.

Solution:
a) 5.00 am; b) 5.30 am; c) 6.00 am; d) 7.00 am; e) 8.00 am
