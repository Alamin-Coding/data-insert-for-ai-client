Question:
Write a short composition in five sentences about 'SM Sultan' answering the following questions. 
a) Where was SM Sultan born?
b) How was the financial condition of his family?
c) Where did he go to study?
d) Where did he travel?
e) What did he do during his travel?

Solution:
SM Sultan
SM Sultan was born in Narail in 1923. The financial condition of his family was not good at all. He went to Kolkata to study. He travelled around Asia and Europe. He painted rivers, trees, villages and the people during his travel.
