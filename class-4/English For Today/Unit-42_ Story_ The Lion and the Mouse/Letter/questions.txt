Question:
Suppose, you are Antu. Your friend Samun wanted to know how you spend your weekend. Now, write a letter to him about it.

Solution:
8/G, Elephant Road, Dhaka
10 February, 2025
Dear Samun,
Take my cordial love. Yesterday I got your letter. You wanted to know how I spend my weekend. Now I am going to tell you about it.
On weekends, I wake up at 7 a.m. After breakfast, I work in my garden. I have a nap at noon after lunch. I visit my aunt and uncle in the afternoon. I play in the yard with my cousins till 5 p.m. In the evening, I watch TV or read story books. At night, I go to bed at 11 o'clock.
No more today. Write me soon about your weekend.
Yours ever,
Antu
