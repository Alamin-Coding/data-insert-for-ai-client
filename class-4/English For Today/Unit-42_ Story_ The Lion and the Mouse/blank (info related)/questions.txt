Question:
Our school is going to arrange the cultural programme next week. The programme will start tomorrow at (a) _____. The students have to be present in the campus by (b) _____. The chief guest will arrive at around (c) _____. He will inaugurate the ceremony at (d) _____. The function will continue up to (e) _____.

Solution:
a) 11:00 am; b) 9:45 am; c) 10:15 am; d) 10:45 am; e) 1:30 pm.
