Question:
Write a short composition on 'The Story of a Lion and a Mouse'.

Solution:
The Story of a Lion and a Mouse
Once upon a time there lived a big and strong lion in a jungle. All the animals of the jungle feared the lion. A mouse was playing near the lion. Suddenly, she ran onto the lion's nose and woke him up. This disturbed the sleeping of the lion. The lion became extremely angry. He threatened to eat up the mouse. The mouse prayed to the lion to let her go. She promised to help the lion. The lion did not believe her. But he eventually freed the mouse. One day, the mouse saved the lion from a trap. Thus they became friends to each other.
