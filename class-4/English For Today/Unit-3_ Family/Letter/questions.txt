Question:
Suppose, you are Farabi. Now, write a letter to your new friend Rudra about your family.

Solution:
Pirojpur
January 7, 2025
Dear Rudra,
How're you? Today, I'm writing you about my family in brief.
There are six members in our family. My father, Mr. Afnan Zaman, is a doctor. My mother, Mrs. Naila Zaman, is a teacher. My younger sister Lima is in Class two. We go to school together. Our parents help us in our studies. My grandfather is Mr. Asaduzzaman and grandmother is Mrs. Shahida Zaman. They love us very much. We also love them.
No more today. Please, write me soon about your
family. With love,
Farabi
