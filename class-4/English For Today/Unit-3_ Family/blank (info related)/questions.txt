Question:
My elder brother reads in class five. He wakes up early in the morning at (a) _____ . He says his prayer at (b) _____ . He takes his breakfast at (c) _____ . He goes to school at (d) _____ . He returns home at (e) _____ .

Solution:
(a) 5:00 a.m.; (b) 5:10 a.m.; (c) 7:15 a.m.; (d) 7:30 a.m.; (e) 11:00 a.m.
