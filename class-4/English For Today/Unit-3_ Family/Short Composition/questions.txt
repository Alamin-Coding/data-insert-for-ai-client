Question:
Write a short composition in five sentences about 'Your Parents'. (Remember to use capital letters, full stops and correct spelling.) 10

Solution:
My Parents
My parents are Altaf Khan and Sabiha Moni. They are very affectionate to me. My father is 40 years old and my mother is 36 years old. My father is a businessman and my mother is a housewife. They take care of me. I also respect and love them very much.
