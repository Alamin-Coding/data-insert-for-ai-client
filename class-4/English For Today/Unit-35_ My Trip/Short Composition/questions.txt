Question:
Write a short composition about "Visiting Your Village Home" answering the following questions. Write at least five sentences.
a) Where is your village home?
b) Who live there?
c) When did you visit your village home?
d) Who did you go with?
e) What did you do there?

Solution:
Visiting My Village Home
My village home is in Barishal. My grandparents, uncles and cousins live there. I visited my village home during the vacation of Eid ul-Fitr. I went there with my parents and younger brother. I enjoyed the delicious foods made by my grandmother there. It was a very enjoyable trip.
