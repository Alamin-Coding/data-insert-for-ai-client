Question:
You are Muzahid. Your date of birth is 6th August. Write a letter to your friend Kamal inviting him to attend your birthday party.

Solution:
Mirpur, Dhaka.
2 August, 2025
Dear Kamal,
Accept my cordial love. I hope you are in good health. You will be glad to know that 6th August is my 9th birthday. My parents are arranging a party on this occasion. So I invite you to attend the function. If you come, I shall be very glad.
No more today. Wishing you the best of luck.
Lovingly yours,
Muzahid
