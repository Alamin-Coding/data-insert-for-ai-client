Question:
Today my English class will start at (a) _____. The Math class will start at (b) _____. Bengali class ends at (c) _____. There is a tiffin break at (d) _____. Our school breaks up at (e) _____.

Solution:
(a) 8 a.m.; (b) 8:30 a.m.; (c) 9:30 a.m.; (d) 9:30 a.m.; (e) 11 a.m.
